## Controller & translation notes

**Source preservation:** Keep original N64 tab text unchanged. Modern translations are separately labeled and do not overwrite historical notation. The actual remake supports "Swap Ocarina-Playing Controls"; the user's selection is **Classic** (four face buttons plus the right bumper).

**User-tested Classic mapping, with Xbox-style labels on the physical 8BitDo Ultimate 2C:**

| Original N64 note key | Nintendo on-screen prompt in Classic | Actual physical 8BitDo (Xbox-style legend) | Pitch on N64 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A` (note key; **not** physical Xbox A) | `R` | **RB** | D4 |
| `Cd` = C-Down | `B` | **A** | F4 |
| `Cr` = C-Right | `A` | **B** | A4 |
| `Cl` = C-Left | `Y` | **X** | B4 |
| `Cu` = C-Up | `X` | **Y** | D5 |

**Important:** The Nintendo prompt labels and physical Xbox-style labels are *reversed in pairs*: Nintendo A/B vs Xbox B/A; Nintendo X/Y vs Xbox Y/X. **Verified example:** Song of Storms is original `A Cd Cu A Cd Cu`, Nintendo `R B X R B X`, physical 8BitDo **`RB A Y RB A Y`** (user-tested).

**Historical pitch modifiers (N64):** `Z` lowers a note one semitone; `R` raises a note one semitone; `U`/`D` analog stick shift one **whole tone** up/down; horizontal stick motion adds vibrato. The N64 `R` sharp modifier is **not** interchangeable with the remake's RB, which is mapped to the baseline D note in Classic. Whether the remake supports pitch modifiers and how they map is **not verified**. Do not label a modified melody "confirmed playable" on the remake until tested.

**Separate notation systems:** In some *Animal Crossing* town-tune FAQs, lowercase/uppercase letters represent different octaves, `z`/`Z` means a rest and `-` a sustain. These codes are *not* N64 `Z` triggers. Moving town tunes to OoT requires a new, verified arrangement; do not mechanically substitute the letters for controller buttons.

**Correction to an earlier exploratory Adam's Song conversion:** The prior in-chat mapping of musical `E` to stick-up+Y and `C` to stick-down+Y was incorrect for the original N64 pitch reference. A hypothetical **D4-relative** conversion would use `E4 = stick-up + RB` and `C4 = stick-down + RB`, if the current remake supports the same pitch modifiers. Musical `A4 = physical B`, and `G4 = stick-up + physical A`. This is a *theoretical* mapping, **not** a playtested remake transcription, and does not settle Animal Crossing's octave notation.

### Source-check corrections for Gemini's report

- **OOT-001 is dated February 11, 1999,** on GameFAQs, not March 14, 1999. It contains a *James Bond Theme* transcription, but its title alone does not establish that it is a *GoldenEye 007* game-score cue.
- The source contains historical typos, inconsistent numbering and punctuation. Gemini normalized or truncated portions of several sequences (some `[span_...]` citation artifacts appeared in its pasted table). **Always read the source file for definitive historical notation**, not Gemini's formatted table.
- Gemini's explanation incorrectly assigns a one-semitone pitch shift to vertical analog stick input; original Zelda references document **whole-tone** vertical stick shifts and **semitone** Z/R shifts. Its generic modern-gamepad and 3DS mapping table cannot replace verified remake-specific controller settings.
- The report's description of widespread Blink-182 OoT circulation is plausible but **not independently demonstrated** by the sources checked; its two purported OoT transcripts remain unproven.
- No additional distinct, verifiable source URL was included in Gemini's original report. The newly indexed OOT-008/009 are follow-up discoveries made while testing its claims.
